How to Reach Our Campus

  • 1
    From Bidar Central Bus Stand: Just 3.5 km away. Frequent auto-rickshaws, city buses, and college transport buses are available directly to Naubad-bhalki Road, Naubad.
  • 2
    From Bidar Railway Station: Approximately 4.2 km. Direct taxi and auto connections via Mailoor - Shahpur road.
  • 3
    College Bus Fleet: Dedicated college buses run daily across all key residential neighborhoods and rural feeder points in Bidar district for day scholars.
