CLINICAL COMPETENCY PROGRESSION

4-Year Clinical Bedside Rotation Roadmap

Structured clinical progression from basic nursing skills to autonomous ICU and emergency critical care nursing.

1

Year 1: Foundations

200+ Clinical Lab Hours

Vital signs monitoring, aseptic sterile technique, bed making, basic CPR, and patient hygiene care.

2

Year 2: Medical-Surgical

450+ Hospital Hours

Pre & post-operative surgical care, IV cannulation, pharmacology drug administration, and wound dressing.

3

Year 3: Specialty Rotations

600+ Clinical Hours

Labor room deliveries, pediatric immunizations, mental health psychiatric assessment, and community health.

4

Year 4: Intensive Internship

850+ Internship Hours

Autonomous nursing shift management, trauma emergency triage, intensive care (ICU/CCU/NICU) and clinical leadership.
