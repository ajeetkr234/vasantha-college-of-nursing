Eligibility Criteria (2026-27)

Program Duration Minimum Qualifications
B.Sc. Nursing 4 Years Passed 10+2 / PUC with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB) with minimum 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/OBC candidates) and English as compulsory subject. Min age: 17 years.
GNM Nursing 3 Years Passed 10+2 / PUC from a recognized board with minimum 40% marks (35% for SC/ST). Candidates with science or arts stream are eligible. Min age: 17 years.

Step-by-Step Admission Process

1

Submit Application Form

Fill out the online application form on this page or visit the college campus office in Bidar directly.

2

Document Verification

Submit 10th & 12th marks cards, Transfer Certificate (TC), Migration Certificate, Aadhaar card, and passport photos.

3

Counseling & Seat Allotment

Meet with the academic counselors to review eligibility, syllabus, clinical hospital postings, and hostel facilities.

4

Confirmation of Admission

Pay admission fees, receive the official admission confirmation letter, and collect your uniform & ID badge.
