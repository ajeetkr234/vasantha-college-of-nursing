STEP-BY-STEP PROGRESSION

The 4-Year Clinical Roadmap

Discover how VASANTHA GROUP OF EDUCATION shapes aspiring students into confident, world-class nursing professionals through structured yearly milestones.

Year 1 � Academic & Clinical Milestone

Fundamental Nursing & Biological Sciences

Students establish strong clinical fundamentals through rigorous hands-on practice in specialized campus simulation labs and conceptual medical coursework.

  • ? Nursing Foundation Lab: Mastering vital signs, bed making, injections, and patient asepsis on advanced adult manikins.
  • ? Anatomy & Physiology Museum: Dissecting anatomical models, organ specimens, and skeletal frameworks.
  • ? Nutrition & Biochemistry Lab: Planning therapeutic clinical diets, infant feeding plans, and community health nutrition.

Year 2 � Academic & Clinical Milestone

Medical-Surgical Nursing & Hospital Wards

Students transition from lab simulation to daily bedside clinical rotations at our 750+ bedded affiliated District Teaching Hospital BRIMS.

  • ? BRIMS Teaching Hospital Rotations: Direct patient bedside nursing in General Medicine, Surgery, and Orthopedic wards.
  • ? Pharmacology & Pathology: Administering medications with 5 rights, IV cannulation, dosage calculations, and blood investigations.
  • ? Pre & Post-Operative Nursing: Sterilization protocols, surgical dressings, catheterization, and post-anesthesia recovery care.

Year 3 � Academic & Clinical Milestone

Maternal, Child & Community Health Postings

Focused training in Obstetrics, Gynecology, Neonatal care, and field postings in urban and rural public healthcare centres.

  • ? Maternity & Child Health Hospital (250+ Beds): Assisting normal deliveries, conducting antenatal checks, and labor monitoring.
  • ? NICU & Pediatric Care: Managing neonatal incubators, phototherapy units, pediatric vaccinations, and growth assessments.
  • ? UHTC Shahpur Gate & RHTC Gunnalli: Organizing rural health screening camps, epidemiological surveys, and family welfare education.

Year 4 � Academic & Clinical Milestone

Critical Care, Internship & Career Placement Launch

The crowning year featuring intensive ICU/CCU postings, a full 6-month clinical internship, and campus placement interviews with top hospital chains.

  • ? Critical Care & Emergency Postings: Ventilator management, cardiac defibrillators, trauma triage, and dialysis unit postings.

  • ? 100% Placement Drives & NCLEX/OET Prep: On-campus recruitment with Apollo, Manipal, Narayana Health, Aster DM, and global exam mentoring.

OUR COLLEGE

VASANTHA GROUP OF EDUCATION, Bidar

VASANTHA GROUP OF EDUCATION, serving for over 43 years (ESTD. 1984), is dedicated to providing quality nursing education with a strong focus on practical training, ethical values and community service. Our institution aims to shape skilled, empathetic and confident nurses who can contribute to better healthcare and a healthier society.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance regarding recognized courses, clinical hospital postings, hostels, and 2026-27 admissions.

Yes, VASANTHA GROUP OF EDUCATION is fully recognized by the Government of Karnataka (G.O. No. MED:39:MME:2019), approved by the Karnataka State Nursing Council (KSNC No. 1211/2019-20), and affiliated to Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS Bangalore, Affiliation Order: RGU/AFF/Fr/397/2019-20). Degrees and diplomas awarded are accepted across India and internationally.
B.Sc. Nursing (4 Years Degree): Candidates must have passed 10+2 / PUC with Physics, Chemistry & Biology (PCB) with a minimum 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/OBC candidates) and English as a compulsory subject. Candidate must be at least 17 years old by December 31st.

GNM Nursing (3 Years Diploma): Candidates must have passed 10+2 / PUC in Science or Arts with minimum 40% aggregate (35% for SC/ST). Minimum age requirement is 17 years.
Students undergo hands-on clinical rotations at the District Teaching Hospital BRIMS Bidar (750+ Beds), Mother & Child Health Hospital (250+ Beds), Urban Health Training Centre (UHTC Shahpur Gate), Rural Health Centre (RHTC Gunnalli), and more than 50+ affiliated multi-specialty hospital partner networks across Karnataka and neighboring states.
Yes! We provide safe, hygienic, and well-furnished separate hostels for female students on campus with 24/7 security, warden supervision, and clean mess dining serving nutritious food. In addition, our fleet of college buses operates on daily routes connecting all parts of Bidar city and rural sectors for day scholars.
Our Dedicated Placement Cell maintains recruitment partnerships with leading hospital networks including Apollo Hospitals, Manipal Hospitals, Narayana Health, Fortis, Aster DM Healthcare, Max Healthcare, and KIMS. We also provide specialized guidance for international nurse licensing exams such as NCLEX-RN (USA), UK NMC CBT/OSCE, and Gulf DHA/MOH/HAAD.
