OUR COLLEGE

Smt. M. C. Vasantha College of Nursing, Bidar

Smt. M. C. Vasantha College of Nursing, established under the Sabah Education Trust (R), is dedicated to providing quality nursing education with a strong focus on practical training, ethical values and community service. Our institution aims to shape skilled, empathetic and confident nurses who can contribute to better healthcare and a healthier society.
